# Ukame Watch action playbook

_Generated from `data/reference/playbook.json` by `tools/build_all.py`. Do not edit by hand._

> Planning-support actions informed by the cited sources. This is not an official NDMA contingency plan. Drought Emergency and Recovery phases are declared by NDMA using food-security indicators this tool does not have, so the tool never issues them.

Kenya Meteorological Department warnings and NDMA drought bulletins always take priority.

## Drought

### Alert

**Trigger:** 3-month rainfall below 75% of the 1991-2020 normal, OR seasonal outlook probability of a driest-third season of 50% or more

| # | Sector | Action | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordination | Share this draft with the County Steering Group and compare it with the latest NDMA drought early warning bulletin before any decision | [NDMA-BULLETIN](https://knowledgeweb.ndma.go.ke/Content/LibraryDocuments/National_Drought_Early_Warning_Bulletin_Feb_202620260311171955.pdf), [NDMA-PHASES](https://www.un-spider.org/sites/default/files/03_ValerianMicheni_NDMA.pdf) |
| 2 | Water | Run a rapid functionality check of boreholes, water pans and earth dams in the county | [KRCS-DROUGHT-EAP](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/kenya-drought-early-action-protocol-summary-eap2022ke02) |
| 3 | Water | Schedule repairs of broken water points and desilting of water pans while there is still lead time | [KRCS-DROUGHT-EAP](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/kenya-drought-early-action-protocol-summary-eap2022ke02) |
| 4 | Livestock | Alert livestock officers to monitor pasture, water distances and livestock body condition for early decline | [ACAPS-DROUGHT](https://www.acaps.org/sites/acaps/files/products/files/20220331_acaps_thematic_report_kenyaimpact_of_drought.pdf), [NDMA-BULLETIN](https://knowledgeweb.ndma.go.ke/Content/LibraryDocuments/National_Drought_Early_Warning_Bulletin_Feb_202620260311171955.pdf) |
| 5 | Anticipatory action | Check whether early action protocol triggers (e.g. Kenya Red Cross drought EAP) are close to being met and confirm readiness | [KRCS-DROUGHT-EAP](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/kenya-drought-early-action-protocol-summary-eap2022ke02), [NDMA-BULLETIN](https://knowledgeweb.ndma.go.ke/Content/LibraryDocuments/National_Drought_Early_Warning_Bulletin_Feb_202620260311171955.pdf) |

### Alarm

**Trigger:** 3-month rainfall below 50% of normal, OR two consecutive assessed windows below 75% of normal

| # | Sector | Action | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordination | Request NDMA confirmation of the county phase and activate the county drought contingency plan through official channels | [NDMA-PHASES](https://www.un-spider.org/sites/default/files/03_ValerianMicheni_NDMA.pdf), [NDMA-BULLETIN](https://knowledgeweb.ndma.go.ke/Content/LibraryDocuments/National_Drought_Early_Warning_Bulletin_Feb_202620260311171955.pdf) |
| 2 | Water | Prepare water trucking and water-point repair for areas with the longest walking distances to water | [KRCS-DROUGHT-EAP](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/kenya-drought-early-action-protocol-summary-eap2022ke02), [ACAPS-DROUGHT](https://www.acaps.org/sites/acaps/files/products/files/20220331_acaps_thematic_report_kenyaimpact_of_drought.pdf) |
| 3 | Cash | Prepare cash support for the most affected households so they can buy water when vendor prices rise | [KRCS-DROUGHT-EAP](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/kenya-drought-early-action-protocol-summary-eap2022ke02) |
| 4 | Nutrition and health | Ask the county health department to scale up nutrition screening of children and pregnant or breastfeeding women | [ACAPS-DROUGHT](https://www.acaps.org/sites/acaps/files/products/files/20220331_acaps_thematic_report_kenyaimpact_of_drought.pdf) |
| 5 | Livestock | Coordinate with livestock officers on timely offtake and feed or water support before animals lose condition | [ACAPS-DROUGHT](https://www.acaps.org/sites/acaps/files/products/files/20220331_acaps_thematic_report_kenyaimpact_of_drought.pdf), [NDMA-BULLETIN](https://knowledgeweb.ndma.go.ke/Content/LibraryDocuments/National_Drought_Early_Warning_Bulletin_Feb_202620260311171955.pdf) |
| 6 | Communication | Publish an approved advisory in English and Swahili through county radio and chiefs' barazas | [NDMA-BULLETIN](https://knowledgeweb.ndma.go.ke/Content/LibraryDocuments/National_Drought_Early_Warning_Bulletin_Feb_202620260311171955.pdf) |

## Heavy Rain

### Watch

**Trigger:** Forecast 3-day rainfall total in the next 7 days above the county's 99th-percentile 3-day total

| # | Sector | Action | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordination | Check the latest Kenya Meteorological Department warnings; they take priority over this tool | [KMD](https://meteo.go.ke) |
| 2 | Early warning | Send early warning messages to communities living along seasonal rivers (laggas) and in low-lying areas | [KRCS-FLOOD-EAP](https://www.anticipation-hub.org/Documents/EAPs/EAP2021KE01_Kenya_Floods.pdf) |
| 3 | Preparedness | Confirm the location and readiness of pre-positioned relief items (shelter kits, water treatment, hygiene kits) | [KRCS-FLOOD-EAP](https://www.anticipation-hub.org/Documents/EAPs/EAP2021KE01_Kenya_Floods.pdf) |
| 4 | Infrastructure | Inspect and clear drainage channels and culverts on key roads | [KRCS-FLOOD-EAP](https://www.anticipation-hub.org/Documents/EAPs/EAP2021KE01_Kenya_Floods.pdf) |
| 5 | Health | Alert the county health department to prepare for waterborne disease after flooding, including cholera | [OCHA-FLOODS-2024](https://reliefweb.int/disaster/FL-2024-000045-KEN) |

### Warning

**Trigger:** Observed 3-day rainfall above the 99th percentile in the last 3 days AND more heavy rain forecast

| # | Sector | Action | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordination | Escalate to the County Disaster Management Committee and align with KMD and Kenya Red Cross updates | [KMD](https://meteo.go.ke), [OCHA-FLOODS-2024](https://reliefweb.int/disaster/FL-2024-000045-KEN) |
| 2 | Evacuation | Prepare evacuation of households in flood-prone areas to identified safe sites | [KRCS-FLOOD-EAP](https://www.anticipation-hub.org/Documents/EAPs/EAP2021KE01_Kenya_Floods.pdf), [OCHA-FLOODS-2024](https://reliefweb.int/disaster/FL-2024-000045-KEN) |
| 3 | Shelter and WASH | Distribute water treatment and hygiene supplies to displaced households | [OCHA-FLOODS-2024](https://reliefweb.int/disaster/FL-2024-000045-KEN), [KRCS-FLOOD-EAP](https://www.anticipation-hub.org/Documents/EAPs/EAP2021KE01_Kenya_Floods.pdf) |
| 4 | Access | Map cut-off roads and bridges to plan how relief will reach isolated areas | [OCHA-FLOODS-2024](https://reliefweb.int/disaster/FL-2024-000045-KEN) |
| 5 | Health | Increase disease surveillance and readiness for cholera cases in affected areas | [OCHA-FLOODS-2024](https://reliefweb.int/disaster/FL-2024-000045-KEN) |

## Heat

### Watch

**Trigger:** 3 or more forecast days in the next 7 with apparent maximum temperature above the county's 95th percentile

| # | Sector | Action | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Communication | Issue public heat advice: drink water, avoid midday sun, check on older people and young children | [WHO-HEAT](https://who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789289062930) |
| 2 | Health | Alert health facilities to expect heat-related illness and confirm cooling and rehydration supplies | [WHO-HEAT](https://who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789289062930) |
| 3 | Schools | Advise schools to shift outdoor activities away from the hottest hours and ensure drinking water | [WHO-HEAT](https://who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789289062930) |
| 4 | Livelihoods | Advise employers and outdoor workers, including herders, to schedule heavy work for cooler hours | [WHO-HEAT](https://who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789289062930) |
| 5 | Water | Check that water points can cope with higher demand from people and livestock | [WHO-HEAT](https://who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789289062930), [KRCS-DROUGHT-EAP](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/kenya-drought-early-action-protocol-summary-eap2022ke02) |

### Warning

**Trigger:** 5 or more forecast days in the next 7 above the county's 95th percentile

| # | Sector | Action | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordination | Activate pre-agreed heat actions with the county health department; link the warning to specific responsibilities | [WHO-HEAT](https://who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789289062930) |
| 2 | At-risk groups | Prioritise outreach to older people, young children, people with chronic illness and outdoor workers | [WHO-HEAT](https://who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789289062930) |
| 3 | Health | Ensure cooling in health facilities and schools where possible | [WHO-HEAT](https://who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789289062930) |
| 4 | Surveillance | Track heat-related illness reports during the event to judge whether actions are working | [WHO-HEAT](https://who.int/europe/publications/i/item/9789289062930) |
| 5 | Compound risk | If drought is also at Alert or Alarm, treat water supply as the top priority for people and livestock | [KRCS-DROUGHT-EAP](https://reliefweb.int/report/kenya/kenya-drought-early-action-protocol-summary-eap2022ke02), [ACAPS-DROUGHT](https://www.acaps.org/sites/acaps/files/products/files/20220331_acaps_thematic_report_kenyaimpact_of_drought.pdf) |
