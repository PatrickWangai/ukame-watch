Ask Ukame Watch

The advisory assistant, running entirely in your browser. Code fetches the data from six tools; a small open AI model only writes the wording; code then checks every number and drought level, adds the cited playbook, and holds the result as a draft until a named person approves it.

Planning support only, not an official warning. Kenya Meteorological Department warnings and NDMA drought bulletins take priority. Nothing you type leaves your device: the model runs on your own computer's graphics chip (WebGPU). On the team's laptop the same assistant runs with the larger Qwen 2.5 3B model through Ollama.
